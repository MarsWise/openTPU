How the machine scales

32 active compute shires versus 34 physical Minion shires is a configuration distinction, not a discrepancy. Published chip totals include 1,088 Minion cores. Research kernels typically use 1,024.

Measured board watts by operand pattern

What explains the difference?

  • Zero-word operand gating can suppress deep pipeline activity.
  • Clocked but stable registers still incur clock-distribution energy.
  • Random mantissas, exponents and signs activate different logic.
  • Temperature-dependent leakage contributes to baseline and thermal feedback.
  • A negative floating-point zero may not match a bitwise zero gate.
On the research card, about 36 W board idle at 80 °C is a large contribution. The ratio between dynamic increments is not the ratio between total board watts.

Lessons for openTPU on FPGA

Keep

Explicit tensor command scheduling, data reuse, banked scratchpads, double buffering, performance counters.

Adapt

Operand isolation and clock enable for ternary zeros; compressor or balanced reduction for accumulation, benchmarked against DSP48 mapping.

Do not blindly transplant

ASIC voltage scaling, latch register files, foundry clock-gating cells, an entire thousand-core NoC, or an FP32 Booth multiplier for ternary arithmetic.
