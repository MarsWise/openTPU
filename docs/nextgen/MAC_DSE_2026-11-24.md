# More MACs a cycle on the XC7K480T: estimate (2026-11-24)

Evidence class: SOURCE INSPECTION and arithmetic. No synthesis was run.

## Where the MXU is now

- r5x lean (m8): 1024 int8 MAC a cycle, i.e. 128 rows x 8 columns. At 133.33 MHz that is
  136.5 GMAC/s at peak.
- Resources (routed, 46a18fc1 / ed482f26):
  - DSP48E1: about 75 % of 1920;
  - LUT: about 65-70 %;
  - slices: 84 %.
- Timing margin: WNS +0.019 ns (ed482f26).
- LLM decode is bound by DDR3, not by MACs. Bonsai runs 63 % MAC at 80 % DRAM. More MACs
  would help only compute-bound graphs: prefill, SD 1.5, YOLO, Kokoro's generator and vLLM
  batches.

## Options

| Option | Gain | Fits? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: 12 columns (MCOLS 12) | x1.5 | +512 DSP: about 1950 of 1920 | does not fit |
| B: higher clock | x1.1-1.2 | WNS +0.019 ns at 7.5 ns | no headroom |
| C1: two int4 weights in one DSP48E1 | x2 on int4 layers | yes (see below) | wait for the measurements |
| C2: two fp4 (E2M1) weights in one DSP48E1 | x2 on fp4 layers | only with a cut DSP cascade | wait for the measurements |
| C3: two ternary weights in one DSP48E1 | x2 on ternary layers | yes | decode is DDR-bound: little gain |
| D: two int8 weights in one DSP | x2 | DSP48E1's ports (25 x 18) are too narrow; this needs DSP48E2 (UltraScale) | not possible |

Field widths for option C. With activation a (int8, the 18-bit B port) and two weights packed
into the 25-bit A port as w_hi << s + w_lo, one DSP computes a*w_hi << s + a*w_lo:

- **int4.** Each product takes 12 bits. Over the 128-row cascade the sum grows 7 bits, so 19
  bits. Shift s = 20 puts w_hi in bits 20-23, which fits in 25. P has 48 bits, so there is no
  overflow.
- **fp4 E2M1.** Taken x2 as integers, the weight range is |w| <= 12: 5 bits signed, 13-bit
  products, 20 bits after 128 rows. Then s = 21 and w_hi needs bits 21-25: one bit too many.
  - If the cascade is cut every 32 rows, growth is 5 bits, s = 19 and w_hi sits in bits 19-23,
    which fits.
  - The cost is extraction adders in fabric: 4 segments x 8 columns x 2 fields of 20-24-bit adds,
    plus the weight path at twice the width.
  - Estimate: LUT +15-25k. Timing risk is high, with slices already at 84 %.
- **Both C1 and C2** need twice the weight bandwidth into the array. That does not help
  DDR-bound decode.
- **Today's compute-bound graphs** (SD 1.5, YOLO, Kokoro, SmolVLA resident graphs) take int8
  weights (`resident.Graph.weight`). Option C helps them only after they move to int4 / fp4,
  with quality measured first.

## Decision

No RTL change for the next build. The first step is to measure on the card:

- `r8_sd.cmd`: the UNet's cycles and the MXU busy share;
- prefill tok/s.

Then:

- If a graph is MXU-bound and holds its quality with 4-bit weights, implement C1 / C2: a new
  `HAS_PACK4` parameter in `otpu_mxu`, a CAPS2 bit, the compiler's packed weight rows, and RTL
  tests against the ISA.
- If the measurements show something else (DDR or VPU bound), C is not the remedy.
